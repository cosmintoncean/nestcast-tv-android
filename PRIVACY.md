# NestCast privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

NestCast has two parts: the **NestCast TV** app for Android TV and **NestCast Server** for Windows. The developer does not run any server for them and **does not collect, receive or store any data about you**. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your devices

**On the TV**, the app stores, only on the TV:
- the names and addresses of your PCs;
- which videos you have watched and where you stopped;
- which person you chose in "Who's watching";
- a random ID that identifies this TV to your own PCs;
- cached poster links.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

**On the PC**, NestCast Server stores its settings (port, folders), a cache of picture thumbnails, a list of TVs you allowed to share your screen, and, if you connect MediaLog, an encrypted access token. It reads your video and picture files only to show them to your own TV.

Your files are streamed directly from your PC to your TV over your home network. They are not uploaded anywhere.

## Connections to other services

Only when the feature is used:

- **TVmaze (api.tvmaze.com).** To show a poster for a TV series, the TV app, and the server when MediaLog is connected, sends the cleaned-up show title taken from a folder or file name. No account, device ID or personal information is sent. Personal pictures and videos are never looked up. TVmaze's own policy applies to those requests.
- **MediaLog (optional).** If you connect your own MediaLog account in NestCast Server, the server sends the title, season/episode and time of what you finished watching, so MediaLog can mark it as watched. This is off until you connect it, and you can disconnect at any time.
- **GitHub (NestCast Server only).** The "Check for updates" menu asks GitHub for the latest release. GitHub sees your IP address as with any web request.
- **Google Cast (optional).** When you cast a PC screen to a Chromecast, the PC and the Chromecast talk to each other over your home network. The picture and sound do not go through any server of ours.

## Permissions

The TV app uses network access to reach your PCs and the services above. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage on the TV.

## Children

NestCast is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
